## Repeated Market Pattern
- No single app is best at all three layers: discovery, planner control, ride-time UX.
- Calimoto wins route discovery framing.
- Scenic wins ride-time UX and CarPlay-like execution.
- Kurviger wins planner control granularity.
- REVER wins community and route-library framing.

## Implication for REVV
- Do not choose one app wholesale.
- Use `Calimoto for discovery`, `Scenic for ride UX`, `Kurviger for advanced shaping`, `REVER for future community/save layer`.
- Keep REVV differentiated on `fun + flow + residential` and `quality / character / explanation`.
